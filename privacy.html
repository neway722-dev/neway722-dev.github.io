Jax Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Who uses Jax Bot

Jax Bot is a personal tool used only by its developer. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data it accesses

Jax Bot asks only for permission to manage calendars that Jax Bot itself created. It cannot see or change any other calendar in the Google account.

It reads and writes the events on its own calendar to keep them in step with a private Notion task list: event titles, times, durations and descriptions.

Where the data goes

  • Event details are stored in the owner's private Notion workspace and on the owner's private server.
  • Task text may be processed by Anthropic's Claude AI to understand the owner's requests.
  • Data is not sold, shared with advertisers or used for any other purpose.

Jax Bot's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the calendar Jax Bot created removes its events.
